# Brand visuals from Arcadia

Material moved here from Arcadia Principal's inbound area on 5 October 2026 as untriaged input for the website's brand and visual design. It is not a trade and carries no instruction. The website decides what to adopt, and anything it leaves unused can be deleted.

## Contents

- `design-notes.md` - a reusable visual style prompt and brand system for Knowledge Islands imagery: archipelagos, isometric islands, colour, tone and composition guidance. Written in June 2026 for generating diagrams, logos, slide graphics and illustrations.
- `concepts/` - seven AI-generated concept images from 9 and 10 May 2026: four UUID-named renders and `image1.png` to `image3.png`. They are early explorations of the island imagery, unlabelled and not reviewed.
- `background-tiles/` - sixteen `knowledge_islands_tile_NN.png` background tiles from 9 May 2026, generated as a seamless set for page or slide backgrounds.
